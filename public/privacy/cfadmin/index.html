Privacy Policy

CloudHUD: DNS & Edge Admin — an independent app for managing Cloudflare accounts

Android package: com.sassyconsultingllc.cloudhud

Effective Date: November 25, 2026   Last Updated: November 25, 2026

Summary

  • We run no server for CloudHUD. We do not receive your Cloudflare API token, your AI key, your Cloudflare account data or your AI chats.
  • The app sends requests straight from your device to Cloudflare, to Google if you choose Gemini, and to Google Play for purchases.
  • No advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting kit of ours.
  • We do receive what you email to us, and the order records and app statistics that Google Play gives developers.

Who We Are

Sassy Consulting, LLC (“we”, “us”) publishes CloudHUD (the “App”). We are the controller of the limited personal data described under “Information We Receive”. Our address and contact details are at the end of this policy.

Information Kept on Your Device

  • Cloudflare accounts: each account’s name, API token and, if provided, email address, held in encrypted secure storage backed by the Android Keystore.
  • AI settings: your chosen AI provider and, if you add one, your Google Gemini API key, held in the same secure storage.
  • App settings and Pro status: theme, notification preferences, whether you have seen the upgrade screen, and a record of your Pro status (plan, product ID, date, and whether it came from a promo code), held in app storage.
  • Held in memory only: your AI chat history, and the list of changes you made during the session, which you can review and export. Both are discarded when the App closes.

AI Content

Do not put passwords, secrets or personal information in AI questions, or in scripts you send for AI editing. Cloudflare handles Workers AI requests under Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy. Google handles Gemini requests under Google’s Privacy Policy and the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service. If your key uses Google’s unpaid service, Google may use what you send, and the responses, to improve its products, and human reviewers may read them. Google applies its paid-service data terms to users in the European Economic Area (EEA), Switzerland and the United Kingdom.

Location, Clipboard, Screenshots and Notifications

  • Location (optional): when you create a storage resource, you can ask the App to suggest a Cloudflare region from your location. Android asks you to allow approximate location. The App turns the coordinates into a region code on your device. Coordinates are not stored or sent anywhere. Only the region you use is sent to Cloudflare with your request.
  • Clipboard: if you choose to paste a map link or coordinates, the App reads the clipboard at that moment and processes it on your device. Copy buttons place the value you pick on your clipboard.
  • Screenshot detection: on supported Android versions, the App is told that you took a screenshot so it can mask secrets. It does not receive the image.
  • Notifications (optional): alerts are created on your device. We send no push messages, and the App does not request a push token.

Information We Receive

  • Messages to us: your email address or phone number and what you tell us, used to answer you.
  • Google Play order records: order number, product, price, date, subscription status, and buyer location such as country, region and postal code, used to manage purchases and refunds and to keep accounts. We do not receive your card details.
  • Google Play statistics: install, performance and crash reporting that Google collects and makes available to developers, according to your Google settings.

If you are in the EEA or UK, our legal bases are performance of a contract (purchases and support), legal obligations (tax and accounting) and legitimate interests (support, fixing problems and preventing fraud). We keep messages for as long as needed to resolve them and keep a business record, and order records for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Sharing and International Transfers

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose it only to providers that run our email and business records for us, when the law requires it, or as part of a merger or sale of our business. We are based in the United States, so information you send us is processed there. Cloudflare and Google process data in many countries under their own policies and transfer safeguards.

Deleting Your Data and Android Backup

  • Remove an account in Settings to delete its token from the App. You can also revoke the token in the Cloudflare dashboard.
  • Clear your Gemini key in the AI assistant’s settings.
  • Clearing the App’s storage, or uninstalling it, deletes its data from your device.
  • If Android backup is turned on, Google may include the App’s data in your device backup. The keys that decrypt stored credentials stay in your device’s Keystore. You can manage backups in your Google account.
  • To have us delete information you sent us, email info@sassyconsultingllc.com.

Security

The App connects over HTTPS and keeps credentials in encrypted secure storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Use a screen lock, give your API token only the permissions you need, and revoke it if your device is lost. If a breach affects personal data we hold, we will notify you and regulators as the law requires.
