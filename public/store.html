Questions

How does checkout work?

Pick a product, enter your email, and you're handed to Lemon Squeezy to pay. The moment payment clears, your license key is generated and emailed to you, and shown on the confirmation page. Card details never touch our servers.

Do I need a SassyMCP commercial license?

Only if you use it for work. Personal projects, learning, and hobby use are free with every tool. If you use SassyMCP in your job, on client work, or across a team, you need one $99 commercial seat per person (one-time, perpetual). You can evaluate it at work free for 30 days first. Full plain-English terms are in LICENSING.md.

Is this really one-time? No subscription?

Yes. You buy once and own that product plus every future update forever. Nothing recurs and nothing expires.

Does a SassyMCP purchase unlock tools?

No. Every tool group ships unlocked for everyone, with no key. A commercial license buys the right to use SassyMCP for work; the supporter license is a tip that shows a badge. Neither changes which tools you get. Download the full product free on GitHub.

Where's my license after I pay?

On the confirmation page and in your email. For Sassy-Talk and WinForensics, the download link carries your key automatically. For SassyMCP, activate with sassy_setup_license or the setup wizard. Lost it? Contact us.

Do you have promo codes for friends & family?

Yes. Share your promo code — the recipient enters it on the Sassy-Talk activation screen in the app (same field as a license key). Promo codes are device-bound and capped; they unlock the app without checkout.

Can I get a refund?

14 days, no questions asked, handled through Lemon Squeezy.
