Questions

How does checkout work?

Pick a product, enter your email, and you'll pay through Lemon Squeezy, a well-known payment company. As soon as your payment goes through, your license key shows up on screen and in your email. We never see your card number.

Is this really one-time? No subscription?

Yes. You buy once and own that product plus every future update forever. Nothing recurs and nothing expires.

Do I have to pay for SassyMCP?

No. Every feature is free for everyone, with no key. The $25 supporter license is purely a way to say thanks and help fund development; it shows a supporter badge and doesn't unlock anything. Download the full thing free on GitHub.

Where's my license after I pay?

On the confirmation page and in your email. For Sassy-Talk and WinForensics, the download link carries your key automatically. For a SassyMCP supporter license, enter it in the SassyMCP setup wizard (or ask your AI assistant to run sassy_setup_license). Lost it? Contact us.

Do you have promo codes for friends & family?

Yes, for Sassy-Talk. Share your promo code and the other person types it into the app where it asks for a license key. Each code works on a limited number of phones and unlocks the app for free.

Can I get a refund?

14 days, no questions asked, handled through Lemon Squeezy.
