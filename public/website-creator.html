Features

WordPress, done right for you

WordPress runs a huge share of the web, but setting it up safely is a chore. Website Creator does the boring parts so you can focus on your business.

AI writes the first draft

Pages, blog posts and product descriptions written by Claude, in a voice that sounds like your business — ready for you to tweak.

Locked down from day one

Blocks password-guessing on your login page and closes the common holes hackers look for, following the industry’s standard security checklist (OWASP).

Automatic backups

Your whole site is copied on a schedule, so if something breaks you can roll it back. Keep the copies on your own computer or in cloud storage (Amazon S3, Cloudflare R2 and similar).

Watches for trouble

Tells you if your site goes down, slows down, or looks like it’s under attack — before your customers notice.

Run your site by chatting

Connect it to an AI assistant like Claude and just ask: “add a holiday hours page,” “update my prices.”

It’s just WordPress

Your site is a normal WordPress site, not something trapped in our system. Any web host or WordPress developer can work on it, and you can move it whenever you like.
